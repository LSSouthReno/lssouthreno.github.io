Your privacy

Living Stones South Reno · the Living Stones app and lssouthreno.com · Updated October 19, 2026

The short version. We use your information only to care for you and help you take your next step at Living Stones. We never sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't use tracking or advertising tools. Your My Journey page is private: only you, and the pastors who care for you, can see it.

What we use, and why

InformationWhy
Your name, photo, contact details, and household, from your Living Stones profile in Planning CenterTo show your page and greet you by name.
Your involvement: groups, serving teams, membership, baptism, classes, and events you've signed up forTo show where you are on your journey and suggest a next step.
Whether you give, and how regularly (never amounts)Only to suggest giving as a possible next step. Amounts are never shown in the app.
Your mobile number, and the one-time code we text or email youTo sign you in without a password.
Notes and prayer requests you sendDelivered to your pastor and the prayer team, and kept private in our church records.
Changes you make to your own contact info or photoSaved to your Planning Center profile.
If you serve on a Sunday: your name, team, role and service timeShown to other signed-in members on the Sunday schedule, so everyone knows who's serving. Never your contact details.

Who handles it for us

  • Planning Center keeps our church records. The app reads from it and writes your updates back to it.
  • Google (Apps Script and Workspace) runs the small server behind the app and sends sign-in emails.
  • Twilio sends the sign-in text messages. They receive only your phone number and the code.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files.

Each of these companies may use your data only to provide that service to us.

On your phone

After you sign in, your phone keeps a sign-in pass for up to 90 days, so you don't need a new code every time. It also keeps a copy of your page so it opens quickly on weak Wi-Fi. Sign out removes both from the device and ends the pass on our server.

Your choices

  • See or fix your information. Open Me → Profile in the app, or tell us if something isn't right.
  • Delete your account and data. In the app, open Me → Profile → Delete my account. This signs you out everywhere and sends us a deletion request. Within 30 days we remove your app sign-in and the personal information we hold about you, except the giving records that U.S. tax law requires us to keep. We'll confirm with you when it's done.
  • Ask us anything about your data at brad@lschurches.com.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18 and over). We don't knowingly let children sign in. Any information about children comes from their parents' household records in Planning Center.

Keeping it safe

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in codes expire within minutes. Only people with a church role, such as pastors and staff, can see information beyond their own, and each person sees only what their role needs. The one exception: signed-in members can see the names of people serving on Sundays (name, team, role and service time, nothing else). If something ever goes wrong, we'll tell the people affected promptly.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
